Privacy

Elohim 777 Player plays music that is already on your device. The songs are not uploaded.

Playlists are saved in this browser on your device. They are not sent to a server.

If you tap Sign in inside Pi Browser, Pi shares your Pi username with this page so the app can show that you are signed in. This page does not ask for your Pi password or wallet phrase.

No advertising profile is built from your listening.
