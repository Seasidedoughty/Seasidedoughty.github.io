Load a folder once. This browser remembers it and scans it again the next time you open the player. MP3, WAV, FLAC, OGG, M4A, AAC and Opus play here. Rescan looks for new songs.

Playlists, the queue, volume, and your place in the song stay in this browser. Playback stays paused until you press play.
